# Apex Private Closure Radar — Research & Qualification Standard

## Mission
Find only companies with a current, material, executive-level commercial problem for which Apex Metrics can supply missing external data, historical data reconstruction, specialist data sourcing/licensing/enrichment, or decision-ready analysis. The objective is a real buying conversation and paid project, not a large lead count.

## Territory and target
- United States and continental Europe, including Ireland.
- Exclude the United Kingdom, India, public-sector tenders, and opportunities older than seven days at first discovery.
- Prioritize mid-market and mid-tier public/private companies, PE-backed platforms and portfolio companies.
- Core sectors: consumer goods/CPG, food and beverage, ingredients/flavors/fragrances, healthcare/biotech/CDMO, industrials, EV/OEM/suppliers, logistics/waste, and private equity/M&A.

## Non-negotiable evidence gates
A company is NOT a qualified urgent opportunity unless evidence supports all applicable gates:
1. **Named company and geography** — independently verify the legal/company identity and country. Never label a pan-European result as a country.
2. **Fresh trigger** — dated within seven days for urgent queue; older context may support but cannot create urgency.
3. **Material business consequence** — evidence of revenue/margin/market-share pressure, delayed launch/expansion, customer/supplier disruption, missed acquisition/deal coverage, capacity or regulatory deadline, or a similarly consequential decision.
4. **Data or intelligence gap** — direct evidence that required data is missing, fragmented, stale, inaccessible, unreliable, too slow, not granular enough, or cannot be assembled from internal/historical sources.
5. **External solution plausibility** — explain exactly which external datasets, historical reconstruction, specialist sourcing/licensing, enrichment, or analysis would close the gap and why an internal team cannot reasonably close it in time.
6. **Buyer path** — identify a verified CEO/President/CSO/Chief Strategy Officer/CFO/CMO/CRO/Chief Commercial Officer/Corp Dev/Procurement leader, or a credible route to the accountable executive. Do not invent names or contact details.

A public company event alone (acquisition, expansion, funding, product launch, hiring, restructuring, earnings decline) is a trigger to investigate, NOT proof of a data requirement. Generic hiring, generic AI transformation, generic dashboard needs, and generic partnership announcements are not buying intent.

## Strong evidence hierarchy
A. Direct primary evidence: CEO/C-suite quote, earnings-call transcript, investor presentation, SEC/company filing, official procurement/vendor request, official product/market launch statement, regulatory filing, or company statement explicitly describing the problem.
B. High-quality corroboration: reputable trade/business press with named source, dated customer/supplier/market event, documented operational disruption, or multiple independent sources.
C. Weak discovery only: news aggregation, unsourced commentary, generic trend reports, job postings, inferred pain. Never qualifies alone.

For every lead, preserve source URL, publisher, publication date, exact supporting passage or concise faithful summary, and evidence type. Seek two independent sources for high-value P1 opportunities where possible; at least one must be primary or direct.

## Apex fit test
Map the gap to a tightly scoped paid engagement:
- Historical company/customer/product/channel/price/deal data reconstruction.
- Competitor, customer, supplier, market, pricing, channel, search-intent, or transaction data sourcing and enrichment.
- Consumer/market demand and sentiment analysis.
- Market entry, whitespace, customer/segment mapping, revenue opportunity sizing.
- PE deal sourcing, target screening, commercial due diligence, portfolio intelligence.
- Supply-chain, supplier, import/export, capacity, and geopolitical exposure intelligence.
State the decision the client must make, data required, deliverable, likely executive owner, urgency, and a realistic 2–4 week pilot only where scope supports it. Never invent budget or project value.

## Scoring and labels
- P1 (closure-ready): all six gates supported; clear business consequence; explicit data gap; named or verified buyer path; action deadline within 30 days.
- P2 (investigate now): strong commercial trigger and plausible data gap, but one key proof/buyer detail remains to verify.
- P3 (watch): trigger only or weak/incomplete evidence. Do not present as an urgent requirement.
- Disqualify: no data gap, no material consequence, stale signal, wrong geography, generic hiring, public-sector tender, no identifiable company, or no plausible Apex fit.

Do not fill a daily quota with weak leads. Zero P1 leads is an acceptable and honest result.

## Required report fields
Company; website; verified country; sector; source date; trigger; direct evidence of business pain; direct evidence of data failure/gap; internal-team limitation (only if evidenced); why external data is needed now; Apex work package; executive owner and verification status; contact path; P1/P2/P3; confidence; next action; source links; unknowns and disqualifiers.

## Language and ethics
Never claim a CEO is in pain, an internal team has failed, or a company urgently needs Apex unless public evidence supports that exact conclusion. Separate observed fact, inference, and unknown. No fabricated contacts, budgets, urgency, or intent. Public information only; respect site terms, privacy, and data licensing.
